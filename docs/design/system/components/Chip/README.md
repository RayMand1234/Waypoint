Chips are filters and multi-select preferences, such as trip interests, flight filters or hotel amenities.

Markup: `<button class="wp-chip" aria-pressed="false">`. Toggle `aria-pressed` to select. Selected chips use a midnight-100 fill with a midnight-900 outline and label.

- The consumer provides the label and the pressed state.
- Keep labels to one to three words. Wrap rows with a `space-3` gap.
- Don't use a chip as a primary action.
